Legacy Planning Overview

Legacy planning includes estate planning, healthcare planning, administrative planning, and communications.

Estate planning contributes to your heir's financial well-being. The rest of your legacy planning contributes to your loved one's emotional well-being. It determines how your loved ones experience your end-of-life care, your after-life administrative burden, how your loss impacts their relationships, and how your communications affect how they move forward with your legacy.

Financial planning helps you Live Well. Investment policies help you Invest Well. Legacy planning helps you Leave Well.

Elements of Legacy Planning

Estate

Asset distribution decisions regarding who, when, how:

  • tax- and cost-efficient
  • proper asset titling
  • beneficiary designations
  • maintaining a will
  • use of trusts as applicable
  • designating a financial power of attorney

Healthcare

Express your wishes and relieve your loved-ones by:

  • addressing long-term care needs
  • establishing a living will
  • designating a medical power of attorney

Administrative

Help your heirs conclude your affairs by documenting:

  • financial & legal advisors
  • marriage/divorce/prenup
  • real estate & mortgages
  • insurance policies
  • credit cards & bill pay
  • online accounts, loyalty programs & social media
  • vehicles, jewelry, etc.

Communication

Perhaps most importantly, is leaving a message to your heirs and loved-ones:

  • administrative letter of instruction
  • funeral preferences
  • a last message from the heart to your loved-ones

Benefits of Legacy Planning

Medical

  • Maintain continuity of medical decisions in every state of life.
  • How medical choices to be made if incapacitated (agent or stated)

Control

  • Avoid Probate
  • Maintain Privacy
  • Divide Assets per your wishes
  • Provide instructions to manage

Values

  • Express your love, values, and hopes to and for your loved-ones
  • Transfer wealth across generations on your terms.
  • Help a favorite cause

Taxes

  • Minimize Estate Taxes
  • Minimize Inheritance Taxes
